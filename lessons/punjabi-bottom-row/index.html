Punjabi InScript: the bottom row

The bottom row holds six consonants found in almost every Punjabi sentence and the tippi, the nasal sign. After this lesson you can type simple sentences.

On this row the left hand types three consonants, ਮ, ਨ and ਵ. Until now your left hand has typed only vowel signs, so give these three extra lines.

On the Windows layout the key Z types nothing in Punjabi.

Tippi and bindi

Punjabi has two nasal signs, and they share one key.

  • The tippi ੰ is the key X alone. It is used with mukta (no sign), sihari, aunkar and dulainkar: ਕੰਮ, ਪਿੰਡ, ਮੁੰਡਾ.
  • The bindi ਂ is Shift and X. It is used with the other vowel signs: ਗਾਂ, ਨਹੀਂ, ਮੈਂ.

Both are typed after the consonant and its vowel sign. For ਪਿੰਡ type ਪ, ਿ, ੰ, ਡ. For ਨਹੀਂ type ਨ, ਹ, ੀ, ਂ.

The danda

A Punjabi sentence ends with the danda, the upright line । and not with a dot. It is Shift and the full stop key: hold the left Shift and tap . with the right ring finger.

ਸੀਮਾ ਨੀਲਾ ਪੀਲਾ ਵੀਰ ਕੇਵਲ ਸਵੇਰ

ਕੰਮ ਪਿੰਡ ਰੰਗ ਸੰਗ ਲੰਮਾ ਮੁੰਡਾ ਬੰਦ

ਮੇਰਾ ਨਾਮ ਰਮਨ ਹੈ। ਮੇਰਾ ਪਿੰਡ ਦੂਰ ਹੈ।

ਨੀਲਾ ਰੰਗ ਸਬ ਨੂੰ ਪਸੰਦ ਹੈ।

ਵੀਰ ਜੀ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਕੰਮ ਤੇ ਗਏ।

The sentences use the danda and the full vowel ਏ in ਗਏ, both typed with Shift. ਏ is Shift and S. Try them, or type the lines without those words first.

Type words as wholes

With three rows learnt, look at a whole word and let the hands type it as one movement. ਨਾਮ is left, left, left. ਕਾਮ is right, left, left. Words that stay on one hand feel slower. That comes from the layout and is not a fault in your typing.

Common trouble on this row

  • ਮ and ਨ mixed up. ਮ is the middle finger on C. ਨ is the index finger on V. Type ਮਨ ਨਮ ਮਨ ਨਮ slowly.
  • ਲ and ਸ mixed up. Both belong to the right index finger. ਸ is straight down from ਰ, ਲ is down and to the left.
  • Tippi where a bindi belongs. Remember the rule by the vowel sign: kanna, bihari, lavan, dulavan, hora and kanaura take the bindi.
  • A dot for the full stop. In Punjabi text use the danda.
