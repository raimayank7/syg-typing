Hindi InScript: the top row

The top row adds the most used matra in Hindi, ा, along with ी and ू, and six common consonants. With the home row and this row together you can type a large share of everyday Hindi words.

The keys

English keyTypesFinger
QौLeft little
WैLeft ring
EाLeft middle
RीLeft index
TूLeft index, reaching right
YबRight index, reaching left
UहRight index
IगRight middle
OदRight ring
PजRight little
[डRight little, reaching right
]़ (nukta)Right little, reaching further

The fingers reach up exactly as they do for the English top row. The pattern from the home row continues: matras on the left, consonants on the right.

Short and long matras sit together

Notice how the layout pairs the short and long forms. The left index finger types ि on the home row and ी directly above it. It types ु on the home row and ू above. The left little finger has ो at home and ौ above, and the ring finger has े at home and ै above. If you remember the home row, the top row follows.

The nukta

The key ] types the nukta, the dot below a letter. It is typed after the consonant. For ड़, type ड and then ़. For ज़, type ज and then ़. The dot makes a different sound, so बड़ा and बडा are not the same spelling. Many Hindi words use ड़ and ढ़, so this far key earns its place.

Drill

का की कू कै कौ दा दी दू जा जी

बात हाथ दाद जाग डाक राजा तारा

गीत दीप जीत बीज हीरा पीता टीका

दूर बूट जूता बहू हरा कार काका

पैर बैठक कौर दौड़ पहाड़ बड़ा

बाहर गरीब जहाज किताब दरबार

राजा की किताब पर एक गीत

दादी बाहर बैठी, पापा कार पर

The seventh line has the word एक, which starts with the full vowel ए. That is Shift and S, taught in the Shift lesson. Try it now: hold the right Shift and press S.

The vowel ा changes everything

In English typing the most used letter is e. In Hindi typing the most used key is probably E too, because it types ा. Make sure the left middle finger reaches up for it and comes straight back to the halant key. The pair क then ा, right hand then left, should become one smooth roll.

Common trouble on this row

  • ी typed for ि. Both belong to the left index finger. ि is at home, ी is up. Practise कि की कि की.
  • ब and ह swapped. Both are typed by the right index finger. ह is straight up from र. ब is up and to the left.
  • Forgetting the nukta. Read the word before typing it. ड़ always needs the second key.
