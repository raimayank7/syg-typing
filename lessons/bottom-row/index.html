The bottom row: Z X C V B N M

The bottom row completes the alphabet. It also holds the comma and the full stop, so after this lesson you can type real sentences.

Each finger reaches down

Every finger reaches down and slightly to the right from its home key. As on the top row, the index fingers have two keys each.

Bottom row keyFingerReaches down from
ZLeft littleA
XLeft ringS
CLeft middleD
V and BLeft indexF
N and MRight indexJ
, (comma)Right middleK
. (full stop)Right ringL
/ (slash)Right little;

B is the hardest key on the keyboard for most learners. It sits far from F, down and to the right, and the left index finger must stretch for it. Some people type B with the right index finger. Choose the left, as taught here, and stay with it.

Curl the fingers

To reach down, curl the finger under a little, as if scratching the table gently towards you. Do not pull the whole hand down. The fingers that are not typing stay on the home row.

Drill

fvf fvf jmj jmj fbf fbf jnj jnj

van man ban name move been numb

dcd dcd k,k k,k sxs sxs l.l l.l

can mix box calm exam voice cabin

aza aza ;/; ;/; zoo zip size zero lazy

come back, bring a box, and mix cement.

seven brave men climbed vast mountains.

a quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.

The last line is the famous sentence that uses every letter of the English alphabet. Type it slowly a few times and you have touched all twenty six letters with the correct fingers.

Space after punctuation

Type one space after a comma and one space after a full stop. Do not put a space before them. Two spaces after a full stop is an old typewriter habit and is not needed on a computer.

Common trouble on this row

  • C typed with the index finger. It belongs to the middle finger. The index finger is needed for V and B.
  • N and M mixed up. N is the nearer reach, straight down and left from J. M is directly below J.
  • X and Z feel impossible. The ring and little fingers are weak. Curl them more, and allow the hand to tilt very slightly, but come back to the home row at once.

You now know every letter. The next lesson adds capital letters.
