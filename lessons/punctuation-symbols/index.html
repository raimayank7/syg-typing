Punctuation and symbols

Letters and numbers cover most typing. The rest is punctuation: the small marks that make writing readable and that every email address and web address needs.

Marks on the right of the home row

The right little finger looks after most punctuation keys. From ; it reaches right for the apostrophe, up for P, and further right for the brackets.

MarkHow to type itFinger
;The home key itselfRight little
:Shift and ;Right little, left Shift
'One key right of ;Right little
"Shift and 'Right little, left Shift
?Shift and /Right little, left Shift
- (hyphen)Right of 0 on the number rowRight little
_ (underscore)Shift and -Right little, left Shift
[ and ]Right of PRight little

Symbols on the number row

With Shift, the number keys give symbols. The fingers are the same as for the numbers.

SymbolKey with ShiftCommon use
!1Exclamation
@2Email addresses
#3Numbers, hashtags
%5Per cent
&7And
*8Star, multiply
(9Open bracket
)0Close bracket

These positions are for the US layout, which is the layout of nearly all keyboards sold in India. On a UK keyboard a few symbols, such as @ and ", are in different places. If a symbol comes out wrong, your computer's keyboard setting does not match the keys printed on your keyboard.

Rules for spacing

  • No space before a comma, full stop, question mark, colon or semicolon. One space after.
  • No space inside brackets or quotation marks: (like this), not ( like this ).
  • No spaces around a hyphen in a joined word: well-known.
  • No spaces anywhere in an email address or a web address.

Drill

Who is it? Is it Ravi? No, it is Asha.

"Come in," she said. "Sit down."

It's Ravi's book; it isn't mine.

Note: bring a pen, a pencil and a ruler.

a well-known writer; a ten-year-old boy

asha.verma@example.com ravi_k@example.org

(first) (second) [third] 50% off, 2 * 3

Pay Rs 450 & keep the bill #27 safe!

Email addresses are worth a line of their own

An email address typed with one wrong character goes nowhere, or worse, to the wrong person. Practise the pattern name, at sign, site, dot, ending until @ and the full stop come without thinking. The addresses in the drill use example.com and example.org, names kept for examples, so they belong to no real person.
