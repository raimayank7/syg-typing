Hindi InScript: conjuncts and the number row

Conjunct letters, where two consonants join, are what make Hindi typing look difficult. On InScript they follow one rule, and the common ones also have a key of their own.

The one rule: halant in the middle

To join two consonants, type the first, then the halant ् on D, then the second. The computer draws the joined form.

To getType
क्यक ् य
स्तस ् त
न्दन ् द
च्चच ् च
ल्लल ् ल
स्वस ् व

So अच्छा is अ, च, ्, छ, ा. And हिन्दी, in its older spelling, is ह, ि, न, ्, द, ी.

The two forms of र

The letter र joins in two ways, and the rule above covers both.

  • र after a consonant, as in प्र, क्र, ग्र: type the consonant, halant, र. So प्रेम is प, ्, र, े, म.
  • र before a consonant, the hook on top called reph, as in कर्म, धर्म: type र, halant, then the consonant. So कर्म is क, र, ्, म.

In both cases you type in the order you speak. In कर्म you say र before म, so र comes first, although the hook is drawn above म.

Ready-made keys on the number row

With Shift, the number keys type the most common conjuncts in one press. These are shortcuts: each gives the same result as typing the letters with the halant.

Shift andTypesSame as
3्रhalant, र (use after a consonant)
4र्र, halant (use before a consonant)
5ज्ञज ् ञ
6त्रत ् र
7क्षक ् ष
8श्रश ् र

So क्रम can be typed as क, Shift 3, म. And कर्म as क, Shift 4, म. Without Shift the number keys type the ordinary digits 1 to 0.

Two more keys on this row

  • The key = types the matra ृ, as in कृपा and गृह. With Shift it types the vowel ऋ, as in ऋण and ऋतु.
  • Shift and - types the visarga ः, as in दुःख and प्रायः.

Which way should you type conjuncts?

Use whichever is faster for you, and be consistent. Many typists use the ready-made keys for क्ष, त्र, ज्ञ and श्र, because those four are common and awkward by hand, and type every other conjunct with the halant. The result in the document is identical, and the practice tool here accepts both.
